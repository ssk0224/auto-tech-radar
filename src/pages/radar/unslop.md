## 主な機能と構成（README の原文）

- 原文（README）: It audits the screen and proposes a direction before it edits anything, so you can steer it early.
- 原文（README）: If the screen is already good, it tells you so and suggests a few small refinements instead of redesigning it.
- 原文（README）: Lists every sign of slop with where it lives.
- 原文（README）: Character, world, the one or two moments that carry the color, and a signature detail, all taken from your product.
- 原文（README）: Spacing, color, icons, copy, type, layout, details, visuals and motion, in that order.
